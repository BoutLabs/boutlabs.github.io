# HereNotes Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 19, 2026 · **Last updated:** November 19, 2026

## About this page

This page explains how HereNotes, an iPhone app by Bout Labs, LLC, handles information. The standard Bout Labs terms at the bottom (your rights, retention, security, children, and so on) are part of this policy.

## HereNotes: what happens to your information

### The short version
HereNotes is a private, local-first notes app. Your notes, the places you attach them to, and your photos live **on your iPhone**. If you choose to turn on **iCloud Sync**, that same information is copied through **your own iCloud account** to your other Apple devices. **Bout Labs does not run a notes server and does not receive, see, or store your note content, note locations, or photos.** HereNotes has no account or login, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party analytics or advertising software.

### What HereNotes handles on your device

| Data | What it is | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Notes | Titles, text with formatting, checklists, links | Stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud Sync, also stored in your private iCloud database. |
| Note locations | The coordinates (and optional place name/address) you attach to a note, and a history of where you moved it | Same as above |
| Photos | Photos you choose from your library or take with the camera, attached to a note (HereNotes saves a compressed JPEG copy) | Same as above |
| Your current location | Your position while you use the app, to place a new note and center the map | Used on your device. Saved only if you save it as a note's location. |
| App settings | Whether onboarding is done, your iCloud Sync choice, last map position | Stored on your device only; never synced and never sent to us |

### Permissions HereNotes asks for
* **Location, only while you're using the app.** HereNotes never asks for "Always" location and does not track you in the background. You can use HereNotes without it by placing notes manually on the map.
* **Photos and camera.** HereNotes uses the iOS photo picker (you choose exactly which photos to share with the app) and, if you allow it, the camera, only to attach images to a note.
* **Background updates.** HereNotes does not show notifications. It allows Apple's silent background updates only so iCloud Sync can tell your devices a change is available.

### Apple services HereNotes uses
These involve Apple, not Bout Labs. Apple handles that data under its own policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
* **iCloud Sync (optional; off until you turn it on).** Uses Apple's CloudKit private database (container `iCloud.com.boutlabs.herenotes`). Your notes, locations, and photos are then stored by Apple in your iCloud account. Bout Labs has no access to your private iCloud database and operates no server in that path.
* **Maps, place search, and address lookup.** The map is Apple Maps. When you search for a place, or when HereNotes looks up the name or address for a coordinate, the search text or coordinate is sent to Apple to return results. Bout Labs does not receive it.
* **Diagnostics.** If you've chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give Bout Labs aggregate, non-identifying crash and usage reports. We have not added any crash-reporting or analytics software of our own.

### What HereNotes does not do
* No Bout Labs account, login, or server for your notes.
* No analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting software. No advertising or advertising identifiers.
* No tracking across other companies' apps or websites (HereNotes does not use App Tracking Transparency).
* We don't sell or share your data. We don't have it.
* No third-party software libraries are bundled in HereNotes 1.0.

### Security
Your notes are kept in the app's protected storage on your device (iOS file protection, "complete until first user authentication"). Connections to iCloud and Apple Maps are handled by Apple's frameworks over encrypted connections. HereNotes 1.0 does not add its own passcode or encryption on top of what iOS provides, and we make no claim of end-to-end encryption.

### Deleting your data
* **Delete a note** in the app. It disappears right away, and is **permanently removed after 30 days** (the wait lets the deletion reach all your devices, even ones that are offline). Removing a note also removes its photos and location history.
* **Delete the app** to remove the data stored on that device.
* **iCloud copies:** HereNotes 1.0 has no in-app button to delete everything from iCloud. If you turn iCloud Sync off, copies already in iCloud stay there and on your other devices. To delete HereNotes data from iCloud, use iPhone **Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → HereNotes → Delete Data**.
* Because Bout Labs holds none of your HereNotes data, requests to access, correct, or delete it are handled through the app and your Apple account. If you write to us, we'll explain how, and delete anything you've sent us directly.

### Support
**How to reach us:** use the support form at **https://www.boutlabs.com/support**, or email **privacy@boutlabs.com**. If you email us or use the support form, we receive your email address, name, and what you write. We keep support requests while your request is active and then for up to three years after our last contact (see section 6 below), longer only where needed for legal claims or obligations.

### Children
HereNotes is not directed to children and collects no personal data from anyone. It is distributed with a general-audience age rating, not in Apple's Kids category.

### Legal bases (EEA/UK)
Your notes, locations, and photos are processed on your device and in your own iCloud account; Bout Labs is not the controller of that data. Support emails you send us are handled under our legitimate interests in helping you.

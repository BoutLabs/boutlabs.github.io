# Bout Labs Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 19, 2026 · **Last updated:** November 19, 2026

## About this page

This page explains how Bout Labs, LLC handles personal information when you visit **boutlabs.com**, contact us, or ask for support through it. App privacy pages use the same standard terms, at the bottom of this page.

**How to reach us:** use the [support form](/support/) or the [contact form](/contact-us/), or email **privacy@boutlabs.com**.

## boutlabs.com: what we collect and why

### Summary
When you browse boutlabs.com, we measure traffic with **Cloudflare Web Analytics**, a cookie-free tool that doesn't track individual people. If you send us an inquiry or a support request through our forms, we receive what you type and store it in our CRM so we can reply. We don't sell your information, we don't run advertising on this site, and our site sets no cookies of its own.

### 1. The contact form (`/contact-us/`)
If you submit the inquiry form we receive: your **name**, **email**, **organization**, **job title**, **website**, **team size**, **timeline**, **what you're interested in**, **how you heard about us**, and **your message**. Fields you leave blank aren't sent.

* **How it is sent:** the form sends your entries directly from your browser to **Capsule CRM** (service.capsulecrm.com), our customer-relationship-management provider. It does not pass through a Bout Labs server.
* **Why:** to respond to you and manage the business relationship (legitimate interests, and steps you ask us to take before entering a contract).
* **Who sees it:** Bout Labs, and Capsule as our service provider (see "Service providers" below).
* Please don't put sensitive personal information in the form.
* A short notice appears with the form: *"We'll use your details to reply to you. See our Privacy Policy."*

### 2. The support form (`/support/`)
If you use the support form we receive your **name**, **email**, the **product** you're asking about (HereNotes, Bout Fitness, Booty Babez, or Bout Labs consulting/other), a **subject**, and your **message**. The form works like the contact form: it sends your entries directly from your browser to **Capsule CRM**, where they're tagged "Support." We use them only to answer you, fix problems, and keep a record of the help we gave (legitimate interests; and contract, if you're a customer).

* **Please don't send** passwords, the contents of your notes, payment details, or health information. We don't need them to help you.
* How long we keep support requests is in section 6 of the standard terms below.
* You can also email us at privacy@boutlabs.com; the same retention applies.

### 3. Website analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics)
We use **Cloudflare Web Analytics** to see how many people visit boutlabs.com, which pages they read, and how fast pages load. Cloudflare, Inc. acts as our service provider.

* **No cookies, no tracking across sites.** Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies, localStorage, or similar browser storage, and does not track individual visitors over time or across other websites. Cloudflare says it does not fingerprint visitors using their IP address, user agent, or other data to display analytics.
* **What it records,** each time a page is viewed, using a small script (a "beacon"): the page address, the page that referred you, your browser, device type, and operating system, a country-level location derived from your connection, and page-speed (performance) timings.
* **Your IP address:** your IP address reaches Cloudflare as part of the normal web request, but Cloudflare says it discards it and does not store it in its core databases or logs, and does not use it to build a profile of you.
* **Retention:** Cloudflare says it keeps full beacon data for about 7 days, then keeps an aggregated sample (roughly 10%) for longer; dashboards show about six months. We do not have access to individual visitors' data.
* **Where processed:** at the Cloudflare data center nearest you, which may be in a country other than yours, including the United States.
* **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in understanding and improving our website. Because the tool doesn't use cookies or similar technologies, we don't ask for cookie consent for it.
* **Opting out:** there are no cookies to refuse. If you'd rather not be counted, use a browser or extension that blocks analytics scripts (for example, block `cloudflareinsights.com`).

### 4. Fonts (Adobe Fonts)
Our pages load typefaces from **Adobe Fonts** (`use.typekit.net`). When your browser loads a page, it requests the font files from Adobe, so Adobe receives your IP address, browser/device information, and the page address. Adobe uses this to deliver fonts and report usage to us in aggregate.

### 5. Hosting and server logs
The site is hosted on **GitHub Pages** (GitHub, Inc., a Microsoft company). GitHub may log IP addresses and request details for security and to operate the service, under GitHub's own privacy statement. We do not have access to those logs.

DNS for boutlabs.com is hosted at Cloudflare. Pages on **www.boutlabs.com** are served by GitHub and do not pass through Cloudflare's proxy, so a normal page view is not a Cloudflare edge request. A request to the bare domain **boutlabs.com** (without www) is redirected to www by a Namecheap forwarder that runs on Cloudflare. Cloudflare receives the IP address of that redirect request.

### 6. Email
If you email privacy@boutlabs.com we receive your address and message. Our email runs on Google Workspace. Retention is in section 6 of the standard terms below.

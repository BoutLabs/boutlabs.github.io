Quick answers: HereNotes

Where is my data?
On your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud Sync, it also lives in your own iCloud account. Bout Labs doesn’t receive your notes, locations, or photos.
How do I delete a note?
Swipe a note in the list and choose delete. It’s permanently removed after 30 days.
How do I delete my notes from iCloud?
HereNotes 1.0 doesn’t have a “delete from iCloud” button. On your iPhone, open Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → HereNotes → Delete Data. {% comment %}OPEN: verify this path on current iOS before publishing.{% endcomment %}
